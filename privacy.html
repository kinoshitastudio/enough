Privacy Policy

Data Collection

ENOUGH does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension has no servers and makes no network requests.

Local Storage

Your settings — reflection message, pause duration, and hide-metrics toggle — are saved locally using chrome.storage.sync. This data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere outside of Chrome's built-in sync.

Permissions

ENOUGH runs content scripts on social media sites solely to hide numeric metrics from the page. It reads no page content and sends nothing externally.

Third Parties

No analytics, no advertising, no third-party services of any kind.
